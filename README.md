# Campus & University Guide (Üniversite Rehberi)

A modern, student-powered mobile & web campus guide designed to help students discover and navigate university life, facilities, local living, and campus orientation.

## Project Concept & Features

The application is a pure **University & Campus Guide** that provides:

1. **Campus & Facilities Directory (Kampüs & Olanaklar):**
   - Detailed profiles for university faculties, historical red-brick campuses, and dorm sectors.
   - Comprehensive facility directory: Main Library, Sports Bay (Zatoka Sportu), Student Canteens, Health Clinic, Dormitories, and International Office.
   - Hours, exact campus location, student tips, Google Maps directions, and official portal links.
   - Student Clubs & Societies (ESN, Solar Team, Iron Warriors, Radio Żak, Choir).
   - Quick 24/7 emergency & helpline dialer (112, Campus Security, International Office).

2. **Student Life & Survival Guide (Öğrenci Yaşam Rehberi):**
   - Practical survival tips categorized by: Housing & Dorms, Public Transport & Trams, Administration & Student ID, Emergency & Health, Daily Life & Grocery, and Social Life.
   - Tag search, category filters, and expandable guidance cards.

3. **City & Campus Spots (Şehir & Kampüs Çevresi Rehberi):**
   - Curated student hangouts, transit hubs, libraries, shopping centers, and parks in Łódź.
   - Direct integration with Google Maps.

4. **Orientation & Onboarding Checklist (Oryantasyon & Kontrol Listesi):**
   - Step-by-step onboarding checklist (Before Arrival, First Days, During Semester).
   - Visual progress bar showing orientation readiness.
   - Personal campus notebook with persistent storage.

5. **Saved & Bookmarks (Kaydedilenler):**
   - Bookmark essential campus facilities, survival tips, or city spots for one-tap offline access.

## Tech Stack

- **Flutter / Dart** (Cross-platform: Android, iOS, Web, Windows)
- **Material 3 Design** with custom brick & paper campus theme (Dark & Light mode support)
- **Shared Preferences** for persistent local state (bookmarks, checklist, personal notes, theme)
